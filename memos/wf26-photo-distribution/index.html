03Track B, in detail

Sponsor gallery, head-to-head

Fotify covers Track A. For Track B, the real choice is Pixieset vs. Pic-Time vs. SmugMug — it comes down to folder organization, storage headroom, and how premium the sponsor's link feels.

Pixieset Pic-Time SmugMug
Vibe Modern, clean — the "Apple" of client galleries Editorial, premium, dynamic Utility, traditional — a polished Dropbox
Folder depth 1 level — Collections → Sets 1 level — Gallery → Scenes Nested — unlimited sub-folders
Cost at our ~140GB $30–40/mo Advanced (1TB) — Pro's 100GB cap is too tight for us $50/mo Advanced (unlimited) — Professional's 100GB falls short too $15–20/mo Power, unlimited storage
Download control Granular — PIN sets, client-only access Excellent — scene-level sharing rules Good — standard privacy controls
Setup speed Minutes, very intuitive Moderate — more settings to configure Slower — older UI, more hierarchy to build
Recommended

Pixieset wins for sponsors. Presentation is everything here — it makes WF26 look high-end with almost no design effort, and it's intuitive enough that our AV team can upload and organize galleries in minutes without anyone asking for help finding the download button.

Between the 139GB full select and everything else we'll want to keep in the gallery long-term, 100GB doesn't leave headroom — budget for Pixieset's $30–40/mo Advanced tier, not the $20/mo Pro plan.
