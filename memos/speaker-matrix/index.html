How to read the numbers

  • "Open" means an empty numbered slot in the sheet. The Tuesday panel runs 45 minutes and takes two standard slots, so it counts as two filled.
  • I checked these against the Aug 18 to 26 meeting transcripts and Slack. 34 keynotes is the team's number, 16 featured and 18 standard on one stage. Breakouts are 20, not the 30 the sheet implies, because Riverside Suite is an Expo room. The 22 Riverside Ballroom slots belong to Arize. That leaves Adam Nahari, currently parked in "Mixed", without a real slot.
  • Nobody has decided which tier sponsor keynotes go in. Sydney wants them in standard. Kyle, Jesse and swyx lean featured. Either way, the 12 open standard slots are the same pool sales expects to sell, so filling all of them with finance speakers will start an argument.
  • Eight placed speakers carry a because the Speakers NYC tab still lists them as informed, not confirmed. The 23 assumes all eight hold.
  • Don't trust the sheet's own "Current Total / Need to Fill" cells. Leadership breakouts say 1 filled and 9 open, but three names are placed. Workshops have 20 grid cells while the published number is 17, and in the Aug 19 meeting nobody could make 17 add up. By cell count it is 9 open, 3 sponsor holds and 4 "reserved", and nobody has said what reserved means.
